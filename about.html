About OurNanny

OurNanny is an iPhone app for US families who employ a nanny, on their own or in a nanny share with other families. It keeps one schedule everyone sees, tracks each child's hours, splits pay fairly when schedules differ, and tells each family what it owes the nanny on payday.

Who it's for
Parents who employ a nanny, alone or in a share. The nanny gets a free login too.
What it does
Shared schedule and hosting rotation, hours per child, pay split between families, payday totals, sick days and vacations, one-tap Venmo
Platform

What OurNanny isn't

OurNanny isn't a nanny agency, a job board or a payroll service. It doesn't find or employ nannies, and money doesn't move through it. It's for families who already have a nanny, or are setting up a share, to run the week together.

How the pay split works

You set the nanny's total hourly rate for one child and for two or more. OurNanny splits each day at every drop-off and pick-up, applies the right rate to each stretch, and divides it across the children who were there. The nanny share pay guide walks through a full example, with a calculator.
